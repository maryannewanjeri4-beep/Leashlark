About LeashLark

Our Mission

At LeashLark, our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need. We strive to create a safe and loving environment for every animal that comes through our doors.

Meet Our Team

Our dedicated team of volunteers and staff work tirelessly to ensure the well-being of every animal in our care. From medical treatment to socialization, we are committed to providing the best possible care for our furry friends.

Meet Jones

Jones is our lead veterinarian, with over 10 years of experience in animal care. He is passionate about ensuring the health and well-being of every animal that comes through our doors.

Meet Sarah

Sarah is our volunteer coordinator, responsible for organizing and managing our team of dedicated volunteers. She is committed to creating a positive and rewarding experience for everyone involved in our mission.

Together, our team works tirelessly to make a difference in the lives of animals in need. We are proud of the work we do and the impact we have on our community.
